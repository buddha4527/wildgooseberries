THIRUNELLY · WHERE FOREST MEETS VILLAGE

Not a resort built around the forest.
A stay that belongs to it.

Wild Gooseberries brings together the things that make northern Wayanad special: old Kerala architecture, red-earth paths, village fields, tall areca palms, forest edges and the quiet presence of the Brahmagiri hills.

This is designed as a private escape rather than a crowded hotel. Come as a family, a group of friends or a small gathering. Stay together, eat together and explore together.

part of your itinerary.

Stay here and use Wild Gooseberries as your base for temple visits, village life, rivers, trekking and wildlife.

01 · CULTURE

Thirunelly Temple

An ancient Vishnu temple in the Brahmagiri landscape, known for its traditional Kerala architecture and spiritual significance.

02 · MOUNTAINS

Brahmagiri Hills

Explore the mountain landscape around Thirunelly. Trekking can be arranged as an add-on.

03 · WATER

Kalindi / Papanasini

Discover the streams and river landscapes around Thirunelly and take time for a quiet riverside experience.

04 · VILLAGE

Nettara Village Experience

Slow down and see the agricultural landscape, village paths and everyday life around Thirunelly.

05 · WILDLIFE

Tholpetty Wildlife

Tholpetty forms the northern part of Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary, known for rich biodiversity and forest safari experiences.
